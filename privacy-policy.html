Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Note: this is a plain-language starting policy covering the information LAZOVA actually collects through this website. It isn't legal advice — if you want to be certain it fully complies with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act or other applicable law, it's worth a quick review by a local lawyer before you rely on it.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information LAZOVA ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, through three places on this site:

  • Contact form (About page): your name, phone number, subject, and message.
  • Order requests (Buy Now): the product, quantity and price you're ordering. We don't collect payment card details anywhere on this site, because we don't process online payments — orders are confirmed manually over WhatsApp.
  • Newsletter sign-up (Blog page): your email address.

When you use the "Send via WhatsApp" or "Contact Us" buttons on this site, you're taken to WhatsApp with a message pre-filled. Anything you send from there is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, since that conversation happens on their platform, not ours.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify and process an order you've requested
  • To respond to a question or message you've sent us
  • To send you updates if you've subscribed to our newsletter

We do not use your information for anything beyond these purposes.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your details are only used internally by LAZOVA to fulfil your order or reply to your enquiry.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This site does not currently use cookies or analytics tracking. If that changes in the future (for example, to understand which pages are popular), we'll update this policy to explain what's collected and how to opt out.

6. Data Retention

We keep order and enquiry details only as long as needed to process your request and for our own basic record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us using the details on our Contact page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, reach us on WhatsApp at +94 71 344 3701 or by email at info@lazova.com.

Shipping

We deliver island-wide across Sri Lanka. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days from the day your order is confirmed. Delivery times may be slightly longer for remote areas or during peak periods.

Shipping is free on all orders over Rs. 35,000. For orders below that, a delivery charge will be confirmed with you over WhatsApp before your order is finalized.

How Orders Are Verified

LAZOVA doesn't process payments online. When you tap Buy Now on a product, you'll get an order summary with a reference number that you can download as a PDF or send directly to us on WhatsApp. Our team verifies the order details and confirms payment and delivery arrangements with you directly over WhatsApp — nothing ships until that's confirmed.

Returns & Exchanges

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is:

  • Unused and in its original condition
  • In its original packaging, with any tags or protective covers intact

For hygiene reasons, pillows and mattress toppers can only be returned if they arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect — not for change of mind once the packaging has been opened.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo of the item. We'll arrange a replacement or refund once verified.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed via bank transfer, since we don't collect card or online payment details on this site. Refunds are typically completed within 5–7 working days of approval.

Questions About Your Order

For anything shipping or return related, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference number and we'll take it from there.
